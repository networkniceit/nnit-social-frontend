Privacy Policy - NNIT Social Automation

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who we are

NNIT Social Automation is a tool of NNIT - Network Nice IT Tec (owner: Solomon Omomeje Ayodele, Minden, Germany) that lets its owner and its business clients write one post and publish it to their own social media accounts, including TikTok.

What data we process

When you connect your TikTok account we receive your TikTok display name, your TikTok Open ID and an access token. We use the token only to upload and publish the videos that you choose to post. We store the token and Open ID in our database so that you stay connected. Videos you upload are stored temporarily with our media host (Cloudinary) so they can be sent to the platforms you select.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, we do not show it to third parties for advertising, and we do not read your private TikTok content. We only post what you tell us to post.

Retention and deletion

You can disconnect TikTok at any time in Settings of the app, which removes your stored token. You can also revoke access in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and websites. To have all of your data deleted, email networkniceit@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for access, correction or deletion of your data. Write to networkniceit@gmail.com.
